## ⚠ Proxy labels — read first

No public dataset labels a wound as infected. The target is:

| Label | Source |
|---|---|
| `1` | AZH wound tissue — venous, diabetic, pressure, surgical |
| `0` | AZH normal skin |

So this model discriminates **wound tissue from intact skin**, not **infected from uninfected**. Every number below is proxy-task performance. A real infection classifier requires labelled infected IV sites from an IRB-approved prospective collection.

AZH images are also chronic and surgical wounds at close range, not cannula sites — the transfer to early phlebitis is an assumption, not a measurement.

| all | 30 | 0.9885 | 0.9993 |

**This table matters more than the headline score.** The top features by gain are all standard deviations of colour channels, not the erythema distances this module was built around. That means the model separates wound from skin by *colour heterogeneity* — an open wound shows red granulation, yellow slough and black eschar at once, while intact skin is uniform.

Early phlebitis at an IV site looks like the opposite: **uniformly slightly red intact skin** — high erythema, low heterogeneity. A model leaning on heterogeneity would miss exactly the case IntelliCannula exists to catch, while still scoring near-perfectly on this proxy task. Compare the `erythema_only` row against `heterogeneity_only` to see how much of the score survives when the messiness cue is removed.

## Limitations

1. **Proxy target** — wound vs skin, not infected vs uninfected.
2. **Grouped by wound, not by patient** — CV holds every ROI crop of one wound out together, but AZH publishes no patient IDs, so two wounds on the same patient may still straddle folds.
3. **Single source** — all training data is one centre, two cameras. The Medetec external set is the transfer check.
4. **No longitudinal data** — the temporal tracker's alert thresholds are calibrated only against synthetic progressions.
